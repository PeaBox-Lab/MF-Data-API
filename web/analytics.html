Average daily movement

Select a group to see its movement trend.

How this is computed

  • Change = NAV on the day ÷ previous published NAV − 1. NAV is an end-of-day value, not a traded price.
  • Direct/Regular and Growth/IDCW plans are collapsed to one canonical plan before aggregating.
  • AMFI has no sector field. Themes are inferred from scheme names in Sectoral/Thematic categories.
  • Gaps longer than four calendar days are excluded so holidays are not reported as daily moves.
